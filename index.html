Rabia

A private, single-user personal assistant. Not a public service.

Rabia is a personal AI assistant used by one person (its owner) through Telegram. With the owner's explicit permission it reads the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail to create calendar events, summarize email, draft replies and send daily reminders.

Rabia is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not accept sign-ups.
