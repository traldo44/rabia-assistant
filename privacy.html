Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who uses this app

Rabia is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to the public and has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent through Google's OAuth screen, Rabia accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Google Calendar, and related Google Workspace data the owner grants) only to perform tasks the owner requests: reading and summarizing email, drafting replies, and creating or reading calendar events.

How data is used

  • Data is used only to provide features to the owner. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.
  • To generate responses, relevant text may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has configured. It is not used by this app for any purpose other than answering the owner's request.
  • Sending, deleting or archiving email, and deleting calendar events, require the owner's explicit confirmation.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Rabia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored on a private server with access restricted to the owner. No Google user data is stored beyond what is needed to complete a request, except notes and reminders the owner explicitly asks Rabia to keep.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
